Frequently Asked Questions

What types of assets can I track? expand_more
You can track virtually any asset, including trailers, containers, heavy machinery, generators, and even smaller tools using our range of powered and non-powered GPS devices.
How long does the battery last on non-powered trackers? expand_more
Depending on the ping frequency, our long-life battery devices can last up to 5-7 years on a single charge, making them ideal for assets without a power source.
Is the hardware weatherproof? expand_more
Yes, our tracking devices are IP67 or IP69K rated, meaning they are fully protected against dust and can withstand high-pressure water jets and immersion.
Can I set up alerts for unauthorized movement? expand_more
Absolutely. You can create custom geofences around job sites or yards and receive instant SMS or email alerts if an asset moves outside those boundaries.
Does the software integrate with my existing ERP? expand_more
Yes, Fleetmatrix offers robust API integrations, allowing you to sync asset data directly with your maintenance, billing, or ERP systems.
How often does the location update? expand_more
Update frequency is fully customizable. Powered assets can update every 30 seconds, while non-powered assets can be set to update once a day or only when movement is detected.
Can I track engine hours for maintenance? expand_more
Yes, for powered equipment, our devices track actual engine run time, allowing you to schedule preventative maintenance based on real usage rather than calendar dates.
Is there a mobile app available? expand_more
Yes, the Fleetmatrix platform is accessible via a dedicated mobile app for iOS and Android, as well as through any standard web browser.
What happens if an asset is stolen? expand_more
You can switch the device into 'Recovery Mode' to increase ping frequency, providing real-time location data to law enforcement to assist in recovery.
Do you provide installation services? expand_more
While many of our devices are 'plug-and-play' or easy to mount, we have a nationwide network of certified installers available for complex fleet deployments.
