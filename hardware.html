HARDWARE & INSTALLATION

Fleet tracking hardware explained

Clear, honest information about the hardware we install in your vehicles and assets.

VEHICLE TRACKING DEVICES

What goes in your vehicle

We offer two types of vehicle tracking hardware depending on your vehicle type and installation preference.

electrical_services

Hardwired Vehicle Unit

Recommended for most commercial vehicles

Connected directly to the vehicle's 12V or 24V electrical system by a qualified auto-electrician. More secure and suitable for long-term installations in vans, lorries, buses and plant vehicles.

  • check12V and 24V vehicle compatibility
  • checkTamper-resistant installation
  • checkEngine ignition sensing
  • checkInternal backup battery
  • checkOffline data buffering
Installation by certified auto-electricians. Costs discussed at quote stage.
usb

OBD-II Plug-In Unit

Suitable for cars, vans and light commercial vehicles

Plugs directly into the OBD-II diagnostic port found in most modern cars and light commercial vehicles. Quick to install without any wiring. Can be self-installed without a technician.

  • checkNo wiring required
  • checkQuick setup — typically minutes
  • checkWorks with most 2003+ vehicles
  • checkPowered directly from the port
Less tamper-resistant than hardwired units. Best suited for company cars and lighter van fleets.
cell_tower

Multi-Network SIM

Roams across major UK networks for reliable coverage across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.

satellite_alt

Multi-Constellation GPS

High-precision GPS using multiple satellite systems for improved location accuracy in challenging environments.

storage

Offline Buffering

Internal memory stores journey data when mobile signal is unavailable and syncs automatically on reconnection.

build

12-Month Hardware Warranty

All Fleetmatrix tracking hardware comes with a standard 12-month warranty. Replacement terms discussed per agreement.

ASSET TRACKING DEVICES

Non-powered asset trackers

For trailers, plant, generators and other equipment that doesn't have a vehicle power supply.

battery_full

Battery Life

Up to 3–5 years of operation based on standard daily location update intervals. Battery status visible in the platform.

water_drop

IP67 Weatherproof

Water and dust resistant enclosures for construction sites, agricultural environments and outdoor use.

link

Flexible Mounting

Magnetic mounts, rivets, screws or cable ties depending on the asset type and exposure requirements.

globe_uk

UK & EU Coverage

Multi-network SIM cards for reliable UK tracking and roaming coverage into mainland Europe.

security

Tamper Detection

Light sensor or removal alerts notify you if a device is detached from the asset.

sensors

Movement Detection

Accelerometer detects asset movement and triggers location updates, optimising battery life between events.

DASHCAM HARDWARE

Vehicle cameras

Cameras are installed by our team and connected to the vehicle telematics unit so footage can be linked to driving events.

Forward-Facing Camera

Records HD road footage ahead of the vehicle. Event clips are automatically uploaded when a trigger is detected (e.g. harsh braking or impact).

  • checkHD recording resolution
  • checkWide angle lens
  • checkNight vision capability
  • checkEvent-triggered cloud upload
  • checkLocal SD card loop recording

Inward-Facing Camera (Optional)

Where selected, inward cameras monitor the driver's cab area. Can be configured or disabled depending on your fleet privacy policies. Drivers should always be informed when inward cameras are active.

  • checkConfigurable monitoring settings
  • checkCan be disabled by administrator
  • checkIR low-light capability
If using inward cameras, you must inform drivers that monitoring is taking place and explain the purpose.
