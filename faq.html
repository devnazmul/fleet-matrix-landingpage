FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions about Fleetmatrix

Straightforward answers to the most common questions about our fleet management software.

Vehicle Tracking

How does vehicle tracking work? expand_more
A small telemetry device is installed in each vehicle. The device uses GPS to determine the vehicle's location and sends that information, along with other data such as speed and ignition status, to the Fleetmatrix platform using a mobile network SIM card. You can then view this information from any web browser or mobile device.
How is the tracking device installed in the vehicle? expand_more
We offer two types of tracking hardware. Hardwired units are connected directly to the vehicle's electrical system by a qualified auto-electrician and are generally used for long-term installations. OBD-II plug-in units can be fitted quickly to most cars and vans without any wiring. Our team will advise on the best option for your vehicle types.
Does Fleetmatrix work across the UK? expand_more
Yes. Our tracking devices use multi-network SIM cards that roam across major UK mobile networks, giving reliable coverage across Great Britain and Northern Ireland. Coverage in remote rural areas depends on the mobile network infrastructure in that location. Please ask about European coverage if your vehicles operate internationally.
Can vehicles be tracked during private use? expand_more
This is something fleet operators should consider carefully. Where vehicles are used privately, you may wish to enable a private mode that pauses detailed tracking. Fleetmatrix provides tools to support private-use policies, but fleet operators are responsible for setting appropriate monitoring policies and informing drivers. We recommend reading our Driver Privacy Guide for further guidance.

Driver Privacy & Monitoring

How is driver information handled? expand_more
Vehicle location and journey data can often be linked to an individual driver and therefore constitutes personal data under UK data protection law. Fleet operators act as the data controller for this information. Fleetmatrix acts as a data processor. Operators should ensure they have informed drivers, established a lawful basis and set appropriate retention periods.
Should I tell my drivers about vehicle tracking? expand_more
Yes. As a general rule under UK data protection law, you should inform employees when monitoring is taking place. This means telling drivers that tracking devices are installed, what information is collected, who can access it and why it is being collected. We recommend taking appropriate legal advice on your specific situation. Our Driver Privacy Guide provides further context.
Does Fleetmatrix make my fleet GDPR compliant? expand_more
No. Fleetmatrix provides tools designed to support responsible fleet monitoring and data management, but we cannot make your fleet legally compliant. Fleet operators remain responsible for their own data protection obligations including lawful basis, driver notices, access controls, retention periods and responding to data-subject rights requests. Always seek appropriate legal advice if you are unsure.

Dashcams & Video

Does Fleetmatrix support dashcams? expand_more
Yes. Fleetmatrix supports forward-facing HD dashcams that record road footage and automatically upload short video clips when a defined event occurs, such as heavy braking or a significant impact. Optional inward-facing cameras are also available depending on the specific camera hardware selected.
Is audio recorded by dashcams? expand_more
Some dashcam hardware has the ability to record audio. Whether audio recording is enabled is a decision made by the fleet operator during installation and configuration. If audio recording is active, drivers should be informed. This is a separate consideration to video recording and has distinct privacy implications.
How long is dashcam video stored? expand_more
Event clips triggered by vehicle incidents are retained in cloud storage for 90 days by default. Continuous footage stored locally on the camera's SD card is automatically overwritten as new footage is recorded, unless a specific event has caused a clip to be flagged and uploaded. Retention settings can be configured by the fleet administrator.

Digital Tachograph

Does Fleetmatrix support Smart 2 tachographs? expand_more
Yes. Fleetmatrix supports Smart 2 tachograph vehicles, including remote download of driver card and vehicle unit data files. If you have specific tachograph hardware that you need to confirm compatibility for, please ask our team during your demo.
How often is tachograph data downloaded? expand_more
Remote tachograph downloads can be scheduled to occur automatically at regular intervals, such as daily or weekly, depending on your legal download frequency requirements. You can also trigger on-demand downloads from the platform.

Asset Tracking

Can I track trailers and equipment as well as vehicles? expand_more
Yes. Fleetmatrix supports battery-powered asset trackers designed for non-powered assets such as trailers, generators, skips, plant and equipment. These devices have long battery life (typically 3–5 years at standard ping intervals) and are enclosed in weatherproof IP67 housings for outdoor use.

Pricing & Getting Started

How much does Fleetmatrix cost? expand_more
Pricing is tailored to each fleet based on fleet size, hardware requirements, installation and the specific features you need. We provide itemised proposals so you can see exactly what you're paying for. Get in touch to request a quote or book a demo.
Is there a contract? expand_more
Subscription terms and contract lengths vary depending on the package you choose. We'll discuss this clearly during the sales process so you understand exactly what you're committing to before you proceed. We don't hide contract terms in small print.
How quickly can we get started? expand_more
After your demo and once hardware has been ordered and delivered, most fleets can be onboarded within a few days to a couple of weeks depending on the number of vehicles and installation requirements. OBD-II plug-in devices can typically be set up more quickly than hardwired installations.
