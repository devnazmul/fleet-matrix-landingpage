Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Fleetmatrix Route Optimisation.

What is route optimisation? expand_more
Advanced algorithmic scheduling that calculates the most efficient vehicle journeys by factoring in live traffic, delivery windows, vehicle capacity, driver hours, and operational constraints.
Why is automated route planning important? expand_more
It cuts planning time from hours to minutes, reduces unnecessary mileage and fuel burn, increases daily completed jobs, and improves customer satisfaction.
Can the platform handle high-density multi-stop routes? expand_more
Yes. Fleetmatrix is built to sequence dozens or hundreds of stops per vehicle while strictly observing delivery time windows and payload limits.
How does dynamic dispatch work during unexpected delays? expand_more
Dispatchers can dynamically recalculate routes or reassign pending stops to the nearest available driver when breakdowns, traffic, or cancellations occur.
Do drivers receive live route navigation? expand_more
Yes. Drivers receive clear, sequenced manifests and live navigation handoff directly through the Fleetmatrix mobile app.
Is Fleetmatrix suitable for different fleet types and industries? expand_more
Yes. The platform supports courier delivery, field service, utilities, construction, maintenance, and trade fleets of any size.
