Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is Fleetmatrix vehicle tracking? expand_more
Fleetmatrix uses high-precision GPS and connected telematics hardware with configurable reporting intervals for pin-point accuracy.
How often is vehicle location updated? expand_more
Vehicle locations feature configurable live polling based on your operational needs and device settings, down to second-by-second updates.
How long does GPS hardware installation take? expand_more
Hardware installation is quick and non-disruptive, completed rapidly by certified technicians or your own maintenance crew.
Can I monitor vehicles across multiple locations? expand_more
Yes, Fleetmatrix provides full multi-depot, multi-region management from one unified cloud platform with customizable permissions.
Can I review where a vehicle has been? expand_more
Yes, full journey route replay, breadcrumb tracking, speed analysis, and historical stop logs are stored securely for comprehensive audit review.
What hardware does Fleetmatrix support? expand_more
We support a wide range of hardware including hardwired GPS trackers, OBD-II/CAN-bus devices, AI video dashcams, asset beacons, and digital tachograph units.
