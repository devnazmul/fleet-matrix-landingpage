Privacy Policy

Fleetmatrix UK Ltd — Last updated: August 2026

1. About this policy

This privacy policy explains how Fleetmatrix UK Ltd ("Fleetmatrix", "we", "us") collects and uses personal data through our website (fleetmatrix.co.uk) and our fleet management platform.

Fleetmatrix UK Ltd is registered in England & Wales (Company No. 14892301) and is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO Reg: ZB591024).

2. Information we collect

Website visitors

  • Name and contact details provided through enquiry or demo request forms
  • Browser and device information collected via cookies and analytics tools
  • IP address and approximate location

Platform customers

  • Account details (name, email, business name)
  • Billing and payment information (processed by our payment provider)
  • Fleet and vehicle data entered into the platform

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to sales enquiries and demo requests
  • To provide and operate the Fleetmatrix platform
  • To send product and service updates (with appropriate consent or legitimate interest)
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To improve our website and services through analytics

4. Lawful basis

We rely on the following lawful bases for processing personal data:

  • Contract — processing necessary to deliver our service to customers
  • Legitimate interests — responding to enquiries, improving our services and direct marketing to business contacts
  • Legal obligation — compliance with applicable laws
  • Consent — where we specifically request it (e.g. marketing emails to individuals)

5. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share data with:

  • Cloud infrastructure and hosting providers
  • Analytics service providers
  • Email and communication tools
  • Payment processors

All third parties are bound by appropriate data processing terms.

6. Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, or as required by law. Customer account data is typically retained for the duration of the contract plus a reasonable period for legal and administrative purposes.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or port your personal data, and to object to processing. To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@fleetmatrix.co.uk.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the ICO at ico.org.uk.

8. Cookies

We use cookies on our website. See our Cookie Policy for details.
