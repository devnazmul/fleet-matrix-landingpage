Driver Privacy Notice

Fleetmatrix UK Ltd — Last updated: August 2026

1. Who manages vehicle tracking?

If your vehicle is fitted with a Fleetmatrix tracking device, the tracking has been set up by your employer or fleet operator — not by Fleetmatrix directly. Your employer is the data controller for the information collected about your journeys and driving activity. Fleetmatrix processes this information on their behalf.

2. What information is collected?

Depending on how tracking is configured, the system may collect:

  • Vehicle location (GPS coordinates)
  • Journey history — start and end points, route taken, distance and duration
  • Vehicle speed
  • Engine status (on/off)
  • Driving behaviour events (such as harsh braking, sharp cornering or speeding)
  • Dashcam video footage (where cameras are fitted)
  • Tachograph data (if your vehicle is subject to tachograph regulations)

3. Why is this information collected?

Your employer will have set out their reasons for monitoring in their fleet policy or in a separate privacy notice. Common purposes include:

  • Fleet management and operational efficiency
  • Driver safety monitoring
  • Verification of delivery or job completion
  • Responding to accident or incident reports
  • Vehicle maintenance planning
  • Compliance with legal obligations (e.g. tachograph regulations)

4. Who can see this information?

Access to tracking data is controlled by your employer. Authorised managers or fleet administrators within your organisation can view journey history, location data and driving behaviour summaries. Fleetmatrix staff may access data for support or maintenance purposes only as necessary.

5. Your rights as a data subject

Under UK data protection law, you have certain rights in relation to your personal data, including:

  • Right of access — you can request a copy of personal data held about you
  • Right to rectification — you can ask for inaccurate data to be corrected
  • Right to erasure — in certain circumstances you can ask for data to be deleted
  • Right to restrict processing — in certain circumstances you can ask processing to be limited

To exercise these rights, contact your employer in the first instance, as they are the data controller. You may also contact the ICO (Information Commissioner's Office) at ico.org.uk if you have concerns about how your data is handled.

6. How long is data retained?

Data retention schedules are configured by the fleet operator (the Data Controller) based on their specific operational and legal requirements. Fleetmatrix retains telematics and video event data strictly in accordance with the customer's contracted instructions and chosen retention policies.

7. Private use of company vehicles

If your vehicle can be used privately, speak to your employer about their private-use policy and whether tracking is suspended during private use. Fleetmatrix provides tools to support private-use modes but configuration is controlled by the fleet operator.

8. Questions

If you have questions about how your data is handled, contact your employer's fleet or HR team. For questions specifically about Fleetmatrix as a processor, you can contact us at support@fleetmatrix.co.uk.
