Redefining the future of mobility through connected intelligence.

Fleetmatrix is a technology company building the next generation of cloud-based fleet intelligence — uniting AI, IoT connectivity, and real-time telematics into one platform to drive operational excellence across the globe.

Built by people who understand fleets.

Fleetmatrix was founded to modernise fleet operations through intelligent software. We saw a gap between legacy hardware tracking and the need for actionable, real-time insights. Today, our team of engineers, data scientists, and logistics experts work closely with fleet managers to build tools that solve complex, real-world operational challenges.
