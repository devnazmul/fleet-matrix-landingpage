Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the tracking data?

chevron_right
Our enterprise GPS units provide accuracy within 2.5 meters. In urban canyons, our proprietary dead-reckoning algorithm supplements GPS data using accelerometer inputs to maintain high accuracy.

Do I need special hardware installed?

chevron_right
We offer plug-and-play OBD-II devices for light duty vehicles, and hardwired installations for heavy equipment. We also integrate with major OEM factory-installed telematics (Ford, GM, Volvo).

How does it handle areas with poor cellular service?

chevron_right
Devices store up to 30 days of tracking data locally when out of cellular range. The moment connectivity is restored, all historical data is batch-uploaded to the platform seamlessly.
