Frequently Asked Questions

How often are tachograph downloads performed? expand_more
Downloads are scheduled automatically based on your compliance requirements, typically every 24 hours for driver cards and weekly for vehicle units, ensuring you never miss a legal deadline.
Is the hardware compatible with all vehicle types? expand_more
Yes, our hardware is designed to integrate with all major digital tachograph brands and models across HGVs, LGVs, and buses.
How is the data stored and secured? expand_more
All data is encrypted and stored in secure, ISO-certified cloud servers, meeting all GDPR and legal data retention requirements for transport authorities.
Can I see driver hours in real-time? expand_more
Absolutely. The dashboard provides live updates on driving time, rest periods, and remaining availability for every driver in your fleet.
Does the system alert me to infringements? expand_more
Yes, Fleetmatrix automatically detects potential infringements and sends proactive alerts to managers, allowing for immediate corrective action and training.
How long does installation take? expand_more
Standard installation typically takes less than 45 minutes per vehicle and can be performed by our certified technicians or your own maintenance team.
Can I export reports for audits? expand_more
Yes, you can generate comprehensive, audit-ready reports in multiple formats (PDF, CSV) with just a few clicks.
What kind of support do you offer? expand_more
We provide 24/7 technical support, dedicated account management, and regular compliance training sessions for your staff.
