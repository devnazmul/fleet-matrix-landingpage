Data Processing Agreement

Fleetmatrix UK Ltd — Last updated: August 2026

Important note: This page provides a summary Data Processing Agreement framework. Before relying on this document for commercial purposes, fleet operators should have it reviewed by a qualified UK data protection professional. This document does not constitute legal advice.

1. Introduction and Parties

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the agreement between Fleetmatrix UK Ltd ("Processor") and the Customer ("Controller") who subscribes to Fleetmatrix fleet management services.

  • Processor: Fleetmatrix UK Ltd, 100 Bishopsgate, London, EC2N 4AG. Company No. 14892301. ICO Registration: ZB591024.
  • Controller: The business customer who has entered into a Fleetmatrix subscription agreement.

2. Controller and Processor Roles

The Customer (Controller) determines the purposes and means of processing personal data. Fleetmatrix (Processor) processes personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller.

The Controller is responsible for:

  • Establishing a lawful basis for processing driver and vehicle data
  • Issuing appropriate privacy notices to drivers and other data subjects
  • Setting data retention periods appropriate to their operation
  • Responding to data subject rights requests

3. Categories of Personal Data Processed

Fleetmatrix processes the following categories of data on behalf of Controllers:

  • Vehicle location data (GPS coordinates, speed, direction)
  • Journey records (start/end location, duration, mileage)
  • Driver identification information (where assigned in the platform)
  • Driving behaviour data (harsh braking, acceleration events, speeding)
  • Dashcam video footage (where cameras are installed)
  • Tachograph data files (DDD files from driver cards and vehicle units)
  • Asset location data

4. Data Subjects

The personal data processed relates to the following categories of individuals:

  • Employees and contractors who drive or operate vehicles and assets covered by the subscription
  • Any other individuals captured in dashcam footage

5. Processing Instructions

Fleetmatrix will only process personal data in accordance with the Controller's documented instructions, which include the terms of the subscription agreement, this DPA and any configuration settings applied by the Controller through the Fleetmatrix platform.

If Fleetmatrix is required to process data for any other purpose by UK law, Fleetmatrix will inform the Controller unless prohibited by law from doing so.

6. Security Measures

Fleetmatrix implements appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure or access. These include:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/HTTPS)
  • Encryption of stored data at rest
  • Access controls and role-based permissions
  • Regular security reviews and monitoring
  • Staff training on data protection responsibilities

7. Sub-Processors

Fleetmatrix uses third-party sub-processors to deliver its services. These include cloud infrastructure providers and mapping service providers. All sub-processors are bound by appropriate data processing terms.

Fleetmatrix will notify Controllers of any material changes to sub-processors. Controllers may object to sub-processor changes within a reasonable period. A current list of sub-processors is available on request from support@fleetmatrix.co.uk.

8. International Data Transfers

Fleetmatrix stores data primarily in UK and EU data centres. Where personal data is transferred to countries outside the UK, Fleetmatrix will ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) or UK adequacy decisions.

9. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach affecting Controller data, Fleetmatrix will notify the Controller without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach. Notification will include:

  • Nature of the breach
  • Categories and approximate number of individuals concerned
  • Likely consequences of the breach
  • Measures taken or proposed to address the breach

10. Assistance with Data Subject Rights

Fleetmatrix will provide reasonable assistance to the Controller to enable the Controller to respond to data subject rights requests, including requests for access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability.

Fleetmatrix will not respond directly to data subject requests without prior agreement with the Controller, except where required by law.

11. Data Deletion and Return

At the end of the subscription or on Controller request, Fleetmatrix will delete or return personal data held on behalf of the Controller within a reasonable period. Confirmation of deletion can be provided on request.

Fleetmatrix may retain anonymised or aggregated data that cannot reasonably be linked to individual data subjects.

12. Audits and Records

Fleetmatrix will make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and contribute to audits or inspections conducted by the Controller or an authorised auditor, subject to reasonable notice and confidentiality obligations.

13. Duration

This DPA remains in force for the duration of the subscription agreement and until all personal data processed on behalf of the Controller has been deleted or returned.

14. Governing Law

This DPA is governed by the laws of England and Wales. Any disputes will be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
